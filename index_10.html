The short version

Tax work shouldn't feel like a black box. We keep it plain, keep you in the loop, and explain the why behind what we're doing.

BridgePoint is a tax and accounting practice built around one idea: your time is better spent on your business than buried in your books. Our team partners with owners and individuals on the numbers, the filings, and the questions that come up along the way.

You won't get handed off to a rotating cast of strangers. Kaleb leads every engagement, and we talk to you in language that actually makes sense — no jargon, no runaround.

Founder & Principal
The team, led by

Kaleb Bridgemohan

Enrolled Agent · Founder, BridgePoint Business Solutions

Kaleb is an IRS Enrolled Agent, which means he is federally licensed to represent taxpayers directly before the IRS. He founded BridgePoint after meeting too many business owners and individuals who felt in the dark about their own tax situation.

He leads the BridgePoint team with a simple standard: explain things in plain language, respect the client's time, and stay in it together. Every engagement runs through Kaleb, backed by the team's day-to-day work.

Away from the desk, Kaleb is usually tracking down a golf ball on the fairway or watching football with the family.

My Why

“I became an Enrolled Agent to sit on the same side of the table as my clients and make the tax code make sense. BridgePoint exists so business owners and families never have to feel in the dark about their own money.”
